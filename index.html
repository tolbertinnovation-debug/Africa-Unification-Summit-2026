About the summit

Turning Africa’s shared aspirations into collective action.

The Africa Unification Summit responds to the growing need for deeper continental integration, stronger collective security, and faster implementation of economic frameworks.

Leaders, policymakers, youth, civil society, investors and the African diaspora will convene to advance a practical agenda for unity, cooperation and sustainable development.

  • Forge the Monrovia Declaration on African Unity and Investment
  • Advance the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA)
  • Support AU Agenda 2063 and regional development frameworks
