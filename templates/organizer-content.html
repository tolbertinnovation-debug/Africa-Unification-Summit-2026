Strategic organizer

Federation of Global Diplomacy

Connecting leadership, diplomacy, and action

The Federation of Global Diplomacy (FGD) is an international Pan-African diplomatic leadership institution advancing global dialogue, ethical leadership, peacebuilding, and sustainable development.

FGD provides the Summit’s strategic leadership, coordination, and international partnership mobilization, drawing on its experience in diplomacy, governance, multilateral engagement, and leadership development.
